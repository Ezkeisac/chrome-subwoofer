CHROME SUBWOOFER: THE RECORD

What was measured, what was predicted, what was assumed, and where each
one is kept. The object says nothing. This file says what it can stand
behind.


MEASURED

  Response, isolated subwoofer      data/response_sub_isolated.csv
                                    mains disconnected, 24-bit,
                                    peak -2.8 dBFS, 45 of 45 tones
  Filter chain                      4th order, -3 dB at 57 Hz
                                    slopes 24.4 and 24.6 dB/octave
  Prediction against measurement    3.65 dB mean absolute, 20-80 Hz
  Box model plus filter             1.19 dB mean, 45-110 Hz

  Every level is relative. The recorder was uncalibrated, so spectral
  shape is available and absolute sound pressure is not.


PREDICTED

  From published driver parameters, never measured on this unit.

  Fc 40.27 Hz    Qtc 0.799    F3 36.2 Hz


DERIVED

  Effective volume   39.09 L, using a nominal 3.13 L driver displacement
  Amplifier dial     about 62 Hz, if the receiver's fixed stage is
                     second order at 90 Hz. The manual gives the
                     frequency and not the slope.


ITEMISED

  Materials          $357.84
                     fasteners, adhesive, primer and sealant not costed


NOT RECORDED

  Where the aperture sits on the baffle. The 3D model reads it from the
  photographs.
  How the panels join. There is no cut list.
  The cone depth and dust cap in the 3D model are drawn, not measured.


UNRESOLVED

  32-56 Hz          measurement exceeds prediction by about 3 dB
  Above 120 Hz      measurement falls below the filter line; not analysed
  Driver parameters published, never measured
